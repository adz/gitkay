# GitKay 0.9.0

## Panes say what has the keys

The chrome around a pane now follows **focus**, not the pointer, and every part of it is its own switch in
Settings → Panes:

- **Hover focuses** — pointing at a pane makes it the target for keys, without clicking. Pointing anywhere in it
  counts: its header and the space around its list, not just the list itself.
- **Dim the other panes** — the wash over the panes without the keys, now optional and **off by default**.
- **Highlight its edge** — a brighter edge on the focused pane, independent of everything else.
- **On focus** — Glow, Shadow or nothing, with an intensity (Supernova, Bonfire, Candle, Firefly) and a colour.
- **Outline** — bordered or not, drawn **Subtle**, **Normal**, or **Custom** with your own colour and thickness.
- **Hide the line between panes** — the splitter stays draggable, it just isn't drawn.

Defaults: glow at Firefly, unbordered, hover focuses, no highlight and no dimming.

## Pick a font instead of typing one

The font fields list the fonts installed on this machine, each shown **in its own typeface**, with a sample of the
result beside the field. The mono list only offers fixed-width families, decided by measuring their glyphs rather than
looking for "Mono" in the name. Typing still works, including a comma-separated stack to fall back through.

## Fixes

- **No flash crossing between panes.** Two panes could light at once — one fading out under the other — leaving a
  bright bar down the splitter.
- **No flash between a pane's header and its content.** The strip between them belonged to no part of the pane.
- **The splitter under the pointer** lifts just clear of the hairline behind it instead of filling with the full
  border colour.
- **Subtle, Normal and Custom outlines really differ**: the theme brushes are found once the pane is in the tree, and
  again when the theme changes, instead of all falling back to one grey.
- The build is warning-clean again: `GitError` renders itself from its own declaration, watermarks became placeholder
  text, and the D-Bus test uses the type that replaced `DBusException`.

## Configuration

Settings and UI state live in `~/.config/gitkay` (lowercase) as just `settings.json` and `ui-state.json`; recent
searches and view preferences moved into the UI state. Settings from before 0.8.0 are not carried over.
