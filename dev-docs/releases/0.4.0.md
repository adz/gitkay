# GitKay 0.4.0

## Keyboard: vim motions throughout

The diff now has a **caret** on the focused line, and most of vim's read-only navigation works in the commit list,
the diff and the changed-files list. Press **?** in the commit list (or **F1** anywhere) for the full sheet.

- **Moving the caret:** `h`/`l`, `w`/`b`/`e`, `W`/`B`/`E`, `0`/`^`/`$`, `%` to the matching bracket, and
  `f`/`F`/`t`/`T` to a character with `;`/`,` to repeat. In side-by-side view the caret belongs to one side and
  crosses to the other at a line's edge.
- **Counts:** `3j`, `5w`, `2fx`, `3]c`, `2n`; `10G` / `10gg` go to the 10th commit, or to line 10 of the focused file.
- **Selecting and copying:** `v` selects characters from the caret and `V` whole lines; `yy`/`Y` copy the line,
  `y` plus a motion copies to it (`yw`, `y$`, `yt)`, `y3w`), and `yi`/`ya` copy inside or around a word, WORD,
  brackets or quotes (`yiw`, `ya(`, `yi"`). Without a line to copy from, `y`/`Y` still copy the commit hash / subject.
- **Finding:** `*` / `#` find the word under the caret forwards / backwards; `n`/`N` continue and put the caret on the
  match. In the diff, `?` opens find-in-diff searching backwards.
- **Scrolling and screen positions:** `zz`/`zt`/`zb` put the focused row at the centre, top or bottom;
  `H`/`M`/`L` focus the top, middle or bottom row on screen; `Ctrl+E`/`Ctrl+Y` scroll one row; `PageUp`/`PageDown`
  move a screen.
- **Panes:** `Ctrl+W` then `h`/`j`/`k`/`l` moves between panes, `w`/`W` cycles, `p` goes back. `Ctrl+W o` maximises
  the focused pane (again to restore), `Ctrl+W _` / `Ctrl+W |` maximise its height or width, and `Ctrl+W =` restores
  the layout.

## Diff

- **Horizontal scrolling** for long lines: Shift+wheel, a tilting wheel or a sideways touchpad swipe. Line numbers
  stay put, and the caret keeps itself in view.
- **Collapsing or expanding a file keeps its header where it was.** The view used to jump, most visibly when the header
  was at the top of the screen.

## Also

- `gitkay --help` and `gitkay --version` print in the terminal on Windows too; `--version` shows the release version
  and commit.
- Settings load through Reified's JSON codec; GitKay no longer uses System.Text.Json. Settings files from 0.3.0 load
  unchanged.
- The hints shown while holding Ctrl now name the full shortcut (`Ctrl+3 files · Ctrl+P go to file`).
